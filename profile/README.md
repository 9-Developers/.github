# 9 Developers

### Own your software.

Software you can keep using, on your terms.
9 Developers is the personal umbrella for projects by Ixirsii.

Everything I build follows one of two models:

- **Free and open source:** Free to use, with source code you can inspect, modify, and share.
- **Buy once, keep that version:** Paid software will offer a one-time purchase with a perpetual license for the version you buy.

All of my current projects are free and open source under the BSD 3-Clause license.

## Featured projects

- **[images](https://github.com/9-Developers/images)** — FreeBSD-based CI images powering my Forgejo Actions workflows.
- **[Valorant Randomizer](https://github.com/9-Developers/Valorant-Randomizer)** — An agent and loadout randomizer for Valorant. Let chance pick your next match’s setup.
- **[Yggdrasil](https://github.com/9-Developers/Yggdrasil)** — A suite of PaperMC plugins that enhance the vanilla Minecraft experience.
